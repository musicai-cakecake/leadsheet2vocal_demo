02

WHY THIS MATTERS

任务动机与研究目标Motivation & research goals

LIMITATION 01 TEXT-TO-VOCAL

更细粒度的旋律与歌词跟随Finer-grained melody & lyric following

现有 Text-to-Vocal（Song）模型在复杂、长上下文旋律条件下的生成能力仍有待提高，尚未充分考虑细粒度的旋律与音素联合跟随。

采用更规范的 ABC 记谱法：可读、可编辑，既能由大语言模型生成，也支持手动编写，无需依赖数字音频工作站（DAW），降低用户使用门槛。

Existing text-to-vocal (song) models still need better generation under complex, long-context melody conditions, with fine-grained joint following of melody and phonemes remaining underexplored.

ABC notation provides a structured, readable, editable format. It can be generated by an LLM or written manually without a digital audio workstation (DAW), lowering the barrier to use.

LIMITATION 02 TRADITIONAL SVS

不止是刚性的「人声乐器」Beyond a rigid “vocal instrument”

传统歌声合成（SVS）忠实还原乐谱，高质量结果高度依赖精准的 MIDI 或 f0 曲线。但多数终端用户不熟悉 DAW，也不愿制作精细的 MIDI 序列。

大模型生成或数据集中的 ABC 可能有质量缺陷，音频–音素对齐粗糙，会降低 SVS 效果。采用文本到音乐的生成式范式，允许模型有限地二次演绎，在保留旋律–音素结构的前提下，追求更强的音乐表现力与多样性。

Traditional singing voice synthesis (SVS) faithfully renders a score, relying on precise MIDI or f0 curves for high-quality results. Most end users are unfamiliar with DAWs and do not want to craft detailed MIDI sequences.

ABC scores from models or datasets may contain defects and coarse audio–phoneme alignment that degrade SVS. A text-to-music generative approach allows bounded reinterpretation while preserving melody–phoneme structure, aiming for greater musical expression and diversity.

LIMITATION 03 DEPLOYMENT

专注人声，探索更轻量的应用Vocal-focused, with lighter deployment in mind

多任务通用音乐模型参数量大，通常依赖大型数据中心服务器，并发推理成本高，应用多限于在线场景。

专注人声生成有望减少模型参数量，提高端侧部署可行性、降低用户成本，并拓展到多模态智能体、游戏、社交与陪伴机器人等场景。

Large multitask music models typically require data-center servers, incur high concurrent inference costs, and are mostly used online.

Focusing on vocals may reduce parameter count and improve the feasibility of on-device deployment, lowering costs and enabling multimodal agents, games, social applications, and companion robots.

RESEARCH DIRECTION SPEECH × SINGING

与语音模型融合的可能性A possible bridge to speech models

歌声可以理解为具有丰富音高和响度变化的语音。对于无伴奏生成需求，歌声模型可以探索与语音模型融合，进一步增加应用场景。

Singing can be viewed as speech with rich pitch and loudness variation. For unaccompanied generation, combining singing and speech models is a potential direction for expanding use cases.

MODEL & DATA

模型版本与音频对照Model version & audio variants

CURRENT VERSIONv3仅用于技术可行性验证For feasibility validation only

训练集是小规模数据集，音频总时长只有 1,345 小时，且没有质量筛选，用于内部技术验证与快速迭代调试。

训练和测试音频均为开源音源分离模型提取的人声。分离过程会降低音质，且无法完全去除伴奏，因此数据质量参差不齐，含有大量低质量、残留伴奏与噪声的音频。

The small training set contains only 1,345 hours of audio, with no quality filtering, for internal feasibility validation and rapid iteration.

Both training and test vocals are extracted by an open-source source-separation model. Separation can degrade quality and leave accompaniment behind, so the data include many low-quality recordings, residual instruments, and noise.
