02任务动机Task Motivation

Limitation 1 现有 Text-to-Music 模型的局限：无法演唱指定旋律 Limitation of Existing Text-to-Music Models: Inability to Sing Given Melodies

现有的文本到音乐模型，包括歌词生成歌曲、文本描述生成音乐等系统，不支持旋律（音高）与音素的联合条件输入。这类模型既不具备可控的旋律生成能力，也无法实现长距离下歌声音高与音素的联合跟随。

为解决该问题，本文模型引入了领谱（lead-sheet）条件输入模式。领谱由音素与 ABC 格式乐谱构成，约束生成的人声匹配用户指定的旋律与歌词结构。

除此之外，ABC 记谱是可读、可编辑的文本格式。它既可由大语言模型生成，也支持用户手动编写，无需依赖数字音频工作站（DAW）。

Existing text-to-music models, including lyric-to-song and text-description-to-music systems, do not support joint conditioning on melody (pitch) and phonemes. They lack controllable melody generation capability as well as long-range joint tracking of vocal pitch and phonemes.

To address this limitation, our model introduces a lead-sheet conditioning modality. The lead-sheet consists of phonemes and ABC-formatted musical notation, which constrains the generated vocal output to conform to the melody and lyric structure specified by users.

Furthermore, ABC notation is human-readable and human-editable text format. It can be generated by large language models or manually authored by end-users, without requiring any digital-audio-workstation (DAW) infrastructure.

Limitation 2 SVS 模型作为刚性「人声乐器」，实际应用场景受限 SVS Models as Rigid “Vocal Instruments” with Limited Practical Applicability

现有歌声合成（SVS）模型相当于刚性的严肃的「人声乐器」，会忠实地还原输入乐谱。想要得到高质量的演唱作品，高度依赖精准、高质量的 MIDI 乐谱。

但在真实使用场景下，绝大多数终端用户不会编写 MIDI 乐谱，不熟悉 DAW 工具，也不愿耗费精力制作精细的 MIDI 序列。

与此同时，由大模型生成或是从数据集获取的 ABC 乐谱普遍存在大量质量缺陷，音频-音素对齐效果粗糙。这类低质量输入会显著降低 SVS 系统的生成效果。

为此，本文采用文本到音乐的模型范式，充分发挥生成式模型的创造性与多样性。该方案允许对输入主乐谱进行有限度的二次演绎，在保留用户给定的旋律-音素结构的前提下，产出音乐表现力更强的结果。

类比视频美颜滤镜的思路：即便输入的领谱本身质量不佳，本方法仍可以生成听感悦耳的音乐作品。

Existing singing-voice-synthesis (SVS) models function as rigid “vocal instruments” that faithfully render input musical scores. High-fidelity singing outputs heavily rely on precise, high-quality MIDI scores.

Nevertheless, in real-world scenarios, most end-users cannot author MIDI scores, are unfamiliar with DAW tools, and are unwilling to invest effort in crafting fine-grained MIDI sequences.

Moreover, ABC scores produced by large models or sourced from datasets commonly suffer from severe quality defects and coarse audio-phoneme alignment. Such low-quality inputs degrade the generation quality of SVS systems significantly.

Accordingly, we adopt the text-to-music paradigm to leverage the creativity and diversity of generative models. Our approach permits bounded reinterpretation of input lead-sheet inputs, producing musically expressive outputs that preserve user-specified melody-phoneme structures.

Analogous to the beautification filter for videos, our method can yield aesthetically pleasing vocal outputs even when the input lead-sheet itself is of suboptimal quality.

03对照组介绍Control Group

对照组复用完全一致的歌词输入与提示词，组内各模型参数量、超参数配置严格匹配。

测试样本全部来源于测试集，训练‑测试集以完整乐曲划分，杜绝片段数据泄露问题。

实验样例均为随机选取，没有刻意挑选高质量样本，客观体现模型平均表现。

The control groups reuse exactly the same lyric inputs and prompts, with strictly matched parameter counts and hyperparameter configurations across all models within each group.

All evaluation samples are drawn from the test set. The training and test sets are partitioned at the full-song level to prevent segment-level data leakage.

All experimental examples are selected at random, without deliberately choosing high-quality samples, to objectively reflect average model performance.

Our self-developed LeadSheet2Song model, with M6 as its internal code name. This experiment uses a small-scale training dataset containing only 1,364 hours of audio; M6 underwent only three post-training epochs and is intended solely for early-stage technical validation.

结论范围Scope of conclusion

在这 21 个严格匹配样例上，音高距离、全局节奏距离、速度距离与伪音符编辑距离均支持 M6 整体优于 LeVo2。全局节奏的 onset 与 duration 合计均支持 M6，duration 优势主要来自更接近的全句时长比。

Across these 21 strictly matched samples, pitch, global rhythm, tempo, and pseudo-note edit distances all support an overall M6 advantage over LeVo2. Both onset and aggregate duration components favor M6; the duration advantage is driven primarily by a closer whole-phrase duration ratio.
